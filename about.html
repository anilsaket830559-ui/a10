About Yumster

A small home-cooking site for people who would rather understand dinner than memorise it.

Yumster began as a note stuck to a kitchen cupboard: what goes with what, and what to use when we have run out. It grew into two guides and three tools, and that is still the whole site.

What we publish

The Everyday Cooking Guide covers the habits that make weeknight food taste deliberate. The Flavour Pairing Guide explains why combinations work so you can invent your own. The three tools on the home page answer the questions that came up most often while we were writing them.

How we stay independent

We take no payment, products or gifts in exchange for naming an ingredient or a brand, and our cooking is deliberately brand-neutral. There are no shopping links here. If ads appear, a third party serves them and they have no influence on what we write.

How we test

Everything here is cooked in an ordinary home kitchen with ordinary equipment: one oven, four hob rings and a set of scales. Where a result depends on your pan, your oven or your ingredients, we say so instead of pretending there is one right answer. Our Editorial Policy explains how we research, review and correct what we publish.

Food safety and allergies

Recipes and advice here may involve common allergens such as wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts, soy and fish. Always read labels and adapt to your own needs, and see our Disclaimer for the limits of what general cooking advice can cover.

Tell us when something is wrong

If a swap fails in your kitchen or a tool gives an odd answer, write to hello@yumster.com or use the contact page. Corrections go to the front of the queue.
